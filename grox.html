How to use:

  • • Click on the grid squares to activate/deactivate steps for each drum track
  • • Use the transport controls to play, pause, or stop the sequence
  • • Adjust the BPM slider to change the tempo
  • • Each track has a volume slider on the left
  • • The orange highlight shows the current playing step
